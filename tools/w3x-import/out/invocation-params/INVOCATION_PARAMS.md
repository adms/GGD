Read-only over the source map. Every value carries CONFIRMED / INFERRED / UNRESOLVED.

## 0. Why this exists

`war3map.w3a` says *which model* an ability uses. It says nothing about how that model is
presented: scale, tint, alpha, fly height, facing, animation clip, playback rate, spawn
offset, or lifetime. All of that is in the GUI-compiled JASS, one call per parameter, bound
to `GetLastCreatedUnit()`. Without this dataset a rebuilt effect is the right model at the
wrong size, the wrong colour, the wrong speed, in the wrong place.

## 3. The traps this encodes

1. **`SetUnitVertexColorBJ`'s 4th argument is TRANSPARENCY, not alpha.** `common.j` calls
   `SetUnitVertexColor(u, .., PercentTo255(100.0 - transparency))`. 0 = fully opaque.
   Reading it as alpha inverts every fade in the map. The dataset stores both
   `transparencyPercent` (as written) and the derived `alphaPercent`.
2. **`UnitApplyTimedLifeBJ(duration, buffId, unit)`** — the unit is the *last* argument.
3. **`KillUnit` on a dummy effect unit is not cleanup** — it plays the model's Death
   animation, which is usually the whole payoff. `RemoveUnit` is the silent one.
4. **The natives are absent.** `SetUnitScale`/`SetUnitVertexColor`/`SetUnitFlyHeight`/
   `SetUnitTimeScale` occur zero times; only the `*BJ`/`*Percent` wrappers are used.
5. **Trigger grouping regex.** `^Trig_(.+?)_(?:Conditions|Actions|Func\d.*)$` with no
   catch-all alternative, or `Trig_Love_Surrender_*` collapses into a bogus `Love` bucket.
6. **Scale is multiplicative.** `SetUnitScalePercent(u,250,...)` is 250% of the unit's own
   `usca` base scale, not an absolute size. Both numbers are in the dataset.

## 8. Dummy-effect units resolved through `war3map.w3u`

196 distinct unit rawcodes are spawned as art by the JASS, and **146** of them carry `Aloc` (Locust) — they are visual-only,
un-selectable, and the unit IS the effect. Each entry in `dummyUnits` carries its `umdl`
model, its `usca` base scale (which every `scalePercent` above is a percentage *of*), and
its complete raw `w3u` mod map — no field whitelist, so nothing is silently dropped.

## 9. Findings that are answers, not gaps

- **74 of the 278 abilities that DO have a JASS handler create no art in it at all.** Their handler is pure
  gameplay; every visual they have comes from the object-data art fields. That is a
  definitive answer for those abilities, not missing data — don't go looking for more.
- **464 abilities carry object-data art** but only 204 have per-invocation parameters. For the rest the
  w3a model at its natural size, colour and speed IS the correct reproduction.

## 10. What is deliberately NOT resolved

- **196 trigger groups** create art but have no
  `GetSpellAbilityId()` gate. They are system triggers (revive, shop, tower, on-attack).
  They are kept under `unattributed` with their `unitTypeGates`, tagged UNRESOLVED. Not guessed.
- **307 parameters** inside ability groups could not be
  bound to a specific spawned effect (runtime global, or cross-function flow with more than
  one candidate). They are kept per ability under `unboundParams` with their subject
  expression, so the information is not lost — it is just not falsely attached.
- **No unit conversion is applied.** Distances and heights stay in WC3 world units; the repo
  has three competing WC3→GGD factors (1/36, 11/600, 1/85) and picking one here would bake
  an unverifiable choice into the evidence.
